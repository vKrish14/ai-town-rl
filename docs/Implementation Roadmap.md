## Current evidence

Pre-route-fix results remain in the original result files for history. Current matched results are `results/post_navigation_fix/summary.csv` and raw files below that directory. Across the 15 environment seeds per checkpoint, PPO survival averaged 98.0–98.7% and wellbeing 67.63–73.96; MAPPO survival averaged 98.0–100% and wellbeing 88.01–89.59. The reported t intervals describe environment-seed variation for each fixed checkpoint only; variation across three training seeds remains small-sample and exploratory.

The price-7 stress configuration and raw three-episode-per-mode records are in `experiments/configs/stress_food_price.yaml` and `results/economy_stress_*.json`. In that condition, survival averaged 0% random, 100% rule, 86.7% primary PPO and 100% primary MAPPO. Treat the small seed set as diagnostic. Financial-stability reward ablation artifacts are saved under `models/ablations`, `logs/replicates/ablation_mappo_seed23.jsonl`, and `results/ablations`; training/evaluation use seed 23 and held-out seeds 1001–1005. Full versus ablated outcomes were 83.70/82.86 wellbeing, 94.99/96.64 health, 13.48/11.60 stress, and 88.68/85.81 social satisfaction, with 100% survival/employment in both. Reward totals are not comparable because one component was removed. Interpret differences cautiously because this is one matched training seed and one component.

Three 60-day environment seeds per checkpoint are saved in `results/long_run_*.json`, including all three training seeds. Survival was 100% for the rule baseline and MAPPO checkpoints, and 70–100% for PPO checkpoints; PPO seed 229 survived 90%, 100%, and 100% on seeds 3004–3006. Treat this as descriptive evidence; the sample is still too small to claim robust survival.
