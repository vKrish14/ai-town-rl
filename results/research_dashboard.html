Recorded evaluation outcomes and training diagnostics for the AI Town multi-agent study.

Evaluation conditions

Population 10 · 24 ticks/day · Food price $5 · Reward weights {"employment_acquisition": 0.25, "financial_stability": 0.15, "starvation": 0.3, "survival": 0.5, "sustainable_work": 0.08, "wellbeing_delta": 0.05}

Every chart is generated from the saved evaluation episode rows in results/*.json. Table values show mean ± standard deviation across evaluation environment seeds. This does not measure variation across independently trained checkpoints. Training curves use logs/train_ppo.jsonl and logs/train_mappo.jsonl. These runs do not establish convergence or broad generalization. Reward values should be interpreted with their configured reward components.
